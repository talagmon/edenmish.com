Created 2026-10-08. Built-in image-generation tool, delivered as `storefront/public/assets/booking-tel-aviv-diorama.webp` (900px WebP). Existing package artwork is reused. ## Final generation prompt

Create a premium miniature 3D diorama of Tel Aviv's seaside promenade for a calm delivery-booking website. Square composition on a very pale lavender seamless background (#f7f2fb), Apple product illustration meets sophisticated cozy city-builder game. Isometric camera 35 degrees above: a tiny curved pale stone street winds from lower right foreground to upper left background, white Bauhaus buildings with rounded balconies, two palm trees, a narrow strip of turquoise Mediterranean sea at far left. Restrained purple #5B2A86 delivery accents, warm morning light, beautiful soft ambient occlusion and lavender shadows, tactile matte clay materials. One tiny elegant purple motor scooter with a rear purple delivery box, parked facing along the street in the lower left quadrant, no rider, box has the exact small white word EdenMish. Place architecture primarily around perimeter, open airy central street area so overlays can show a delivery route. Entire composition a floating architectural scale model on a thin rounded pale lavender base with generous margins. No people, no captions, no map labels, no UI, no road text, no logos besides the scooter box EdenMish. Crisp polished professional render, gentle depth, high visual clarity at small size. Not dark, not neon, not a huge metropolis. Single standalone artwork.
